## T1.10 · `repo_count` and `last_activity_at` are **arguments**, and that is the anti-N+1 measure

The projection takes them keyword-only and `list_projects` feeds them from
`Store.repo_counts()` and `Store.project_last_activity()` — one statement each
for the whole list. A projection that reached for a store would be an N+1 the
moment it was called in a loop, which is exactly how it is called.

`test_the_project_list_issues_a_bounded_number_of_statements` traces the read
connection and compares **two fixture sizes**: one project and eight cost the
same number of statements. An assertion at one size cannot tell a constant from
a linear cost, and no assertion on the payload can tell them apart at all.

It reaches the tracing seam through `store._read()`, with a `noqa: SLF001` and a
comment: `Store` deliberately exposes no connection (D33), so measuring the
statement count has to reach past the surface. The alternative — a public
"give me your connection" verb — would cost more than the private access does.

## T1.10 · the two N1 sites are now positive statements

`tests/web/test_fleet_page.py:104` asserted `projects["projects"] == []` on a
fresh install. It now asserts the **whole seeded row**, fields included:
`{"project_id": "unassigned", "name": "Unassigned", "description": "Work that

migration's seeded text, the derived-not-written `last_activity_at`, and the
missing-key-means-zero rule of `repo_counts`, in one assertion.
