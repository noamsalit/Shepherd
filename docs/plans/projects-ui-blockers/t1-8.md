## T1.8 · the deletion landed in someone else's commit, and that is a concurrency finding · owner: router

`git rm` stages immediately. A parallel session on the same branch committed
while T1.8's two deletions sat in the index, so they were swept into
**`09d3d5d "Retire A6 in the plan, and name the risk that actually survived
it"`** — a docs commit that says nothing about deleting a module. Nothing is
lost: the deletion is in history and the three retirement entries are in this
builder's own commit. But the two halves of one decision are now in two
commits, one of which does not mention it.

**The reusable part.** Two agents sharing a branch share an *index*, not just a
tree. A `git rm` — or any `git add` — by one is committable by the other, and
`git commit -m` with no paths takes whatever is staged. Either sessions get
their own worktree, or deletions are performed with `rm` and staged only at
commit time.
